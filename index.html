01 / About

Curiosity,
made tangible.

I’m interested in how ideas become physical systems — how a mechanism moves, how a machine senses, and how engineering can create new ways to interact with the world.

I study Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Edinburgh. My interests are drawn towards robotics hardware, mechanical design, embedded electronics and the creative possibilities that emerge when these disciplines meet.

This website is a starting point, not a finished portfolio. It will grow as the experiments, prototypes and ideas do.
