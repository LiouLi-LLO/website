01 / About

A practical approach
to engineering.

Curiosity is useful. Building, testing and learning from the result is where progress happens.

I’m studying Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Edinburgh, where I’m developing a foundation in electrical systems and engineering principles.

My interests sit at the intersection of mechanical design and electronics. I’m particularly drawn to robotics that interact with the physical world: compact mechanisms, mobile platforms, creative actuation and thoughtfully integrated hardware.

This portfolio is a record of projects, experiments and ideas as they develop. More technical details and results will be added as work is completed.
