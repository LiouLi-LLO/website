01 / About

Curiosity
becomes form.

I’m interested in how ideas become physical systems — how a mechanism moves, how a machine senses, and how engineering can create new ways to interact with the world.

I study Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Edinburgh. My interests are drawn towards robotics hardware, mechanical design, embedded electronics and creative technology.

This website is an open starting point. It is designed to grow with the work, experiments and questions that come next.
