01 / Point of view

The machine is
more than its code.

I’m drawn to the moment an idea becomes a physical object — when geometry, materials, electronics and movement have to work together.

My interests centre on robotics hardware: mechanisms that create motion, compact electrical systems, sensing and the details that turn a collection of components into a working machine.

This site is an evolving notebook of questions, prototypes and design studies. I want it to show not only what is made, but how the thinking develops along the way.

Working principle / 001 Make the invisible tangible. Understand the mechanism. Build it, test it, and let the result change the design.

A sphere that jumps

Exploring how a compact spherical robot might combine rolling locomotion with a spring-driven jump. A study in stored energy, mechanism geometry and landing.
